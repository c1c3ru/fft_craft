There are 2200 Characters in FFT. The most commonly used characters only require 1 byte of storage. However, anything beyond 0xCF requires 2 bytes, and some important characters such as the reflect and zodiac sign characters are in there, but it mostly consists of Eastern Kanji characters.


Character Graphic (35 bytes, 2bpp, 10 width, 14 height)

  • BATTLE.BIN: 0x000E7614
  • WORLD.BIN: 0x0005B8F8


Character Length (1 byte)

  • BATTLE.BIN: 0x000FF0FC
  • WORLD.BIN: 0x000733E0


As the longest English character is 6px long and since a the main character can hold up to 14 characters in his name, the {Ramza} tag used in events should always be considered to be 84px (6 * 14) in length.


Colors

  • 0x0 = Alpha (blank)
  • 0x1 = Darkest
  • 0x2 = Lighter
  • 0x3 = Shadow
